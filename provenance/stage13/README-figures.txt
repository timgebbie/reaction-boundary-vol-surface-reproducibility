Recommended LaTeX usage

  \centering
  \begin{subfigure}{0.48\linewidth}
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/mpl-surface3d-combined-structural-high-z-v3.pdf}
    \caption{Surface view.}
  \end{subfigure}
  \begin{subfigure}{0.48\linewidth}
    \includegraphics[width=\linewidth]{figures/mpl-contour-lines-combined-structural-high-z-v3.pdf}
    \caption{Contour-line view.}
  \end{subfigure}
  \caption{Projected local volatility from the reaction-boundary closure.}
\end{figure}

Axis convention

- m = ln(x/x0): log-moneyness relative to the reference price x0.
- T: maturity in years.
- sigma_loc: projected local volatility.

Profile/regime naming

- liquidity-only: stock dependence generated by L(m).
- resilience-response-only: stock dependence generated by z(m)=nu(m)Delta.
- combined-structural: L(m), z(m), and A_eff(m) vary together.
- low-z: Eq. 22 low-z response approximation.
- high-z: Eq. 22 high-z response approximation.
