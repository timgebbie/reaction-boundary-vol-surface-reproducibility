# Signed phase–shape covariance

The six additive and six projected configurations use two matched inputs and the same 17 operational lags from 32 to 320. The increment covariance is measured from the paths and checked against the expected signed cross spectrum. All 612 predictions fall inside the corresponding design intervals. The relative error in the reconstructed total variance is at most 1.90 × 10⁻¹².

Two additive configurations pass both the local-subdiffusion and total-variance criteria. These are the independent case at input exponent 0.4 and the negatively correlated case at 0.6. In the projected field the signed contribution reverses with forcing placement and its magnitude stays below 0.059% of total variance. No projected case establishes simultaneous scaling throughout the decade. At input exponent 0.6 the new aligned replication also misses the total criterion. Its lower slope envelope reaches 0.884 under this experiment's ensemble and simultaneous interval construction.

Run `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 python -W error scripts/run_phase_shape.py` and `python -W error -m unittest discover -s tests -v` from the repository root. The [path measurements](../../outputs/v2/phase-shape-paths.csv), [design estimates and spectral checks](../../outputs/v2/phase-shape-designs.csv), [whole-window outcomes](../../outputs/v2/phase-shape-window.csv) and [signed fractions](../../figures/v2/phase-shape.svg) include all controls. The [consolidated supplement](../../supplementary-materials/ReactionBoundaryVolSurface-Supplement-v1.6.0.pdf) derives the neutral projection, stable remainder and correlation measurement.
